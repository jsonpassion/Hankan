1. 서비스 소개

한 칸(Hankan, 이하 "앱")은 일본어능력시험 JLPT를 준비하는 한국어 사용자가 한자어의 읽기를 익히도록 돕는, 독립적으로 개발된 iOS 앱입니다. 앱은 JLPT를 시행하는 국제교류기금, 일본국제교육지원협회와 관계가 없습니다. 앱은 N5부터 N1까지의 JLPT 한자어 읽기 문제 4,311개와 표기 문제 3,354개, 한국어 한자음으로 읽기를 설명하는 받침 규칙, 한국 한자음으로 한자를 찾는 기능, 듣기 연습(칸 두드리기)을 제공합니다. 레벨마다 처음 50단어와 받침 규칙 전체는 무료이며, 나머지 단어는 앱 내 구입(이용권)으로 이용할 수 있습니다.

  • 이미 구입한 이용권의 범위는 줄어들지 않습니다.

  • 같은 Apple 계정으로 로그인한 다른 기기나 앱을 다시 설치한 뒤에는 "구매 복원"으로 구입을 복원할 수 있습니다.

4. 콘텐츠, 규칙, 음성

  • 모든 단어, 예문, 음성, 규칙은 앱 안에 들어 있으며 학습에 인터넷 연결이 필요하지 않습니다. 콘텐츠는 앱 업데이트로 추가되거나 수정될 수 있습니다.
  • 단어와 예문을 읽는 음성은 사람이 녹음한 것이 아니라 AI 음성 모델로 미리 만든 것입니다. 일부 단어는 기기에 내장된 음성으로 읽습니다.
  • 오답 보기는 시험의 출제 방식을 본떠 앱이 만든 것이며, 실제 기출문제가 아닙니다. 표기 문제에서 "없는 말"은 사전에 없는 한자 조합이라는 뜻입니다.
  • 받침 규칙과 그 적중률은 앱에 담긴 JLPT 한자어를 대상으로 개발자가 측정한 값입니다. 규칙에는 예외가 있으며, 다른 단어나 문맥에서는 결과가 다를 수 있습니다.

English

1. About the service

Hankan (한 칸, "the App") is an independently developed iOS application that helps Korean speakers preparing for the Japanese-Language Proficiency Test (JLPT) learn how kanji words are read. The App is not affiliated with the Japan Foundation or Japan Educational Exchanges and Services, which administer the JLPT. The App provides 4,311 reading and 3,354 spelling questions on JLPT kanji words from N5 to N1, rules that explain the readings through the Korean reading of each kanji, a kanji look-up by Korean reading, and a listening drill (Tap the squares). The first 50 words of every level and all the rules are free; the other words are available through an in-app purchase (a pass).

2. Free use and in-app purchases

  • Free: the first 50 words of every level and all the rules. Every feature works without limit inside the free range; no feature is sold separately.
  • Paid: from the 51st word on, a pass is needed. A pass is a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription or a lifetime pass; any of them opens every word of all five levels. There is no free trial.
  • The scope of a pass you have already bought is never reduced.
  • Auto-renewing subscriptions (monthly, yearly): unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, a subscription renews for the same period at the same price, and your Apple account is charged within 24 hours before the period ends. You can cancel at any time in iOS Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions, and you keep access until the paid period ends. Deleting the App does not cancel a subscription.
  • Lifetime pass: a one-time, non-consumable purchase. It does not renew and there are no further charges.
  • Price changes: subscription price changes are announced in advance under Apple's policies, and an increase is not applied to existing subscribers without their consent.
  • Prices are those shown in the App Store and may vary by country, exchange rate and promotion.
  • Payment is handled through your Apple account; no sign-up or login is required.
  • Purchases can be restored with "Restore purchases" on another device signed in to the same Apple account, or after reinstalling the App.
  • Refunds, including subscription refunds, follow the Apple App Store's refund policy. The Developer cannot process refunds directly; request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

3. Study data

Your level, rounds, per-word records and trap records are stored on your device and in your own iCloud key-value storage. The Developer collects or stores none of your study data on any server. "Start over" in the App clears the whole study record and cannot be undone.

4. Content, rules and voice

  • Every word, sentence, voice clip and rule is inside the App; studying needs no internet connection. Content may be added or corrected in App updates.
  • The voice that reads words and sentences was not recorded by a person; it was made in advance with an AI voice model. A few words are read by the device's built-in voice.
  • The wrong readings and spellings are made by the App after the way the exam sets its questions; they are not actual test questions. In spelling questions, "no such word" means a kanji combination no dictionary lists.
  • The rules and their rates were measured by the Developer over the JLPT kanji words in the App. Rules have exceptions, and results may differ for other words or contexts.
  • Korean readings of the kanji are based on the Unicode Consortium's Unihan database (© Unicode, Inc., Unicode License v3).
  • The JLPT is administered by the Japan Foundation and Japan Educational Exchanges and Services. "JLPT" is used in the App and its store listing only to say which test the content prepares for; the App is not affiliated with, sponsored, endorsed or approved by either organization.

5. Disclaimer

The App is a study aid and does not guarantee passing any level or any score. Rule rates, trap records and Tap the squares results are practice aids.
